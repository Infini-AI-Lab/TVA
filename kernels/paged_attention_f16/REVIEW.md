For PAGE_SIZE >= 2 the reduction's result is an addition, not a product, and the entry is right. (Line 61 at HEAD_DIM = 1 is not affected: its one-leaf row sum is multiplied by `scale`, not added; line 66's one-leaf `tl.sum(p)` is an `exp`, not a product.)
