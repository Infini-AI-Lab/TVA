## New issues

- **NEW** wording — `docs/SEMANTICS.md` "Programs" (lines 145-157) still says that the semantics "performs a program's atomics in every order their data and control dependences allow", and that in a well-behaved launch no program can observe a reordering "except through atomics, whose every order the semantics allows". Both statements are unqualified. That is the premise that L161 found false across threads, and SEMANTICS.md:841 says so. Fix: say that the dependences are the semantics' tensor-step ones, which are coarser than the device's per-thread ones, and add "outside the exclusion of Atomics (library issue L161)".
