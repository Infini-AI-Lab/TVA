# Library review, round 9, by lr-9b

## Verdicts

- **L4** ACCEPT — Every constant of `Rules.Num.Sm90` holds on TVA's evidence, which I re-ran (five folders, outputs byte-identical) and extended with adversarial runs: `tl.dot` with subnormal operands times large ones, the truncation-window worst case (accumulator `1`, products just below the window: at most 0.123 of `DotF16`/`DotBF16`, 0.436 of the fp8 facts on `wgmma`) and subnormal products on the `ieee` dot, all inside; `DivFull` where it is tightest in ulps (at most 0.62); and the device's fp8 conversions against the Lean rounding that `CvtRn` names (every `float16`/`bfloat16` pattern, 65551 `float32` ones: 0 differ), so the facts are tied to the library's own definition, not only to the evidence script's reference. `Platform.sm90ref` satisfies the facts together with `Compiler.Faithful` (`work/Usability.lean` U8 proves `Claim.Consistent` for a bundle citing 16 of them). The two bounds met exactly are met by construction, as the author says: `Exp2F32`'s `abs` is the flush threshold and flushed results lie just below it (every input checked), and the `ieee` dot's 0.499 is `K·2^-150` against `(K+1)·2^-149` (the atomic's tight bound is likewise met by round-to-nearest ties, and the fact doubles it). No in-scope tutorial needs an unstated item at its pinned configurations (math only on `float32`; dots of `float16`, `float8e5`, `float8e4nv` with the default `max_num_imprecise_acc`; fp8 outputs in range; tutorial 10's mxfp8 and mixed formats). No constant changed meaning.
- **L33** ACCEPT — The lemmas say what their names and docstrings claim (`Tree.eval_err_abs` keeps `eval_err`'s bound; per node `3u`, `4η` from `FloatV.AddRel.err`), use the standard axioms only, and compose with the rules a kernel proof uses: `work/Usability.lean` feeds `safe_sum_bcast_mul`'s result to `sum_bcast_mul_err` with `DotF32Ieee` (U6) and `safe_sumc`'s to `sumc_err_covers` (U7); `tl.sum1c` (tutorial 06's `tl.sum(o * do, axis=1)`) and `tl.cumsumc` (`ScanLeaves.perm`, `sumc_err_list`) follow the same way.
- **L34** REOPEN — `SafeA.atomic` and the integer kit are right (U1, U2: `replay_add_any` resolves at `int32` and `uint64`, and a `tl.atomic_add` step maps the cell by `+ v`), but the float accumulation does not give what a kernel needs. `replay_err` (and `foldl_max_spec`, `foldl_min_spec`, `sum_proj_any`) weight each logged step by a function of the step (`w : AtomicStep → ℚ`), and an `AtomicStep` records only the cell and its old and new values: two programs adding `1.0` and `0.5` to a `float32` cell holding `2^24` both log `⟨.f32, a, 2^24, 2^24⟩`, so no `w` gives each step its own operand, and `replay_err` does not bound the cell against `x₀ +` the sum of the programs' operands; a kernel would have to average the operands of equal steps and prove the step relation convex in `w`. Fix: state the accumulation per position (a list of step–operand pairs, with `Replay` over the steps), with a lemma that merges the programs' operand lists along the interleaving `G` (`proj`) so that the sum over the log is the sum over programs, and the same for the `max`/`min` folds; or record the operand in the logged step.
