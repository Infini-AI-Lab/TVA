# Library review, round 12, by lr-12b

## Verdicts

- **L65** ACCEPT — The new layer (`lean/TVA/Math/Defs.lean`, `Encoding`, `Rounding`, `Arith`, `LittleEndian`) reads as IEEE 754-2019 and the OCP formats. I checked by hand the field decoding and `maxVal` of the seven formats, `roundRat` (quantum `max(⌊log₂|q|⌋, emin) − m`, ties to even, the carry into the next binade, the subnormal range, overflow to `±∞`/NaN/`±maxVal`, toward zero to `±maxVal`), the special operands of `add`, `mul`, `fma`, `div`, `sqrt`, the sign of an exact zero (§6.3, `fma` with a product that cancels `z` included), `maxNum`/`minNum` on signed zeros, `cmp`, and `cvt` of NaN and `±∞` with and without `sat`. The device agrees: with a driver built from the current `Defs.lean` I re-ran 88 of the evidence's 150 tests, three exhaustive `2^32` ones among them (work/results.out); the only differences are the non-saturating fp8 conversions, on exactly the recorded counts. My own test (work/edge.py, work/edge.out: every pair, and for `tl.fma` every triple, of 54 edge values per format, 62 operations: underflow to a signed zero, overflow, subnormal ties, cancellation, `±∞`, quiet and signalling NaNs) finds no difference; part E, `e2m1` and evidence/literals print exactly their recorded outputs. The error lemmas (`FloatErr`, `FusedErr`, `FloatMax`, `Sum`, `SumFused`, `DotLoop`, `Consistency`, `Sm90`) state what LIBRARY.md and their docstrings say (wording below), their hypotheses are dischargeable for the standard formats, and they match the `tl.*` relations they are about (`AddRel`, `MaxRel`, `tl.mul_add`, `tl.dot_add`'s default zero accumulator, `sumWith`). Every fact of `Rules.Num.Sm90` keeps its IEEE meaning and stays true on the new layer (`CvtRn` on every input, from the exhaustive `sat` agreement and `FloatV.cvtSat_of_le`), and `Platform.sm90ref` still satisfies them all. The layer's constants can be evaluated in a kernel proof with `decide +kernel`, standard axioms only (work/usable.lean; plain `decide` gets stuck on a finite rounding). The docstrings cite only evidence/float-layer, with no "ported", "conformance" or "v1"; the coordinator's release check passes `build`, `library` and `audit`. The list of changed constants is an adequate umbrella: it does not name `TVA.Lang.Scaled` (`MXFmt.value`, `scaledValue`), `TVA.Lang.Random` (`scale32`) or `TVA.Lang.Desc` (`fMinMax`), but "every `tl.*` float operation" covers them, and the digests re-open their items anyway.

## New issues

- **NEW** wording — The float layer says the value of `±∞` is unused, which is false. `lean/TVA/Math/Encoding.lean`'s module docstring says `toRat` of `±∞` and NaN is "the value the formula gives, beyond `maxVal`, which no statement of the library uses"; the round-12 notes say the same, `FloatV.toRat` (`lean/TVA/Lang/Scalar.lean`) calls it "meaningless for NaN and `±∞`", and `Format.toRat` (`Defs.lean`) "meaningful when it is finite". But `Format.maxNum`, `minNum` and `cmp` order `±∞` through it, and so do `tl.maximum`, the comparisons and `FloatV.sortKey`; `FloatV.le_iff`, `lt_iff`, `eq_iff`, `toRat_max`, `negInf_toRat_le` and `Tree.eval_maxRel` state facts about infinite operands through it; `toRat_lt_iff_orderKey` says "infinities included". Fix: say that `toRat` of `±∞` is `±2^(emax+1)`, beyond `maxVal`, which orders the infinities correctly; that only a NaN's value is meaningless; and that every error bound also concludes finiteness.
- **NEW** wording — `lean/TVA/Lemmas/FloatMax.lean`'s module docstring lists `FloatV.max_mem`, `le_max_left`, `le_max_right` ("and `min`"). The module has no `le_max_left` or `le_max_right`, so those names resolve to Mathlib's lattice lemmas. Fix: name what it provides, `toRat_max`/`toRat_min` and `MaxRel.le`/`MaxRel.ge`.
- **NEW** wording — Two docstrings of the layer claim more than the formats allow. `lean/TVA/Lemmas/FusedErr.lean`: the hypothesis `u ≤ 1/16` holds for "every format the library covers", but `float8e5` has `u = 1/8` and `e2m1` `1/4`. `FloatV.ofRat` (`lean/TVA/Lang/Scalar.lean`): "`±∞` on overflow", but it gives NaN in `e4m3` and `±maxVal` in `e2m1` (`DType.ofRat .f8e4nv` uses it). Fix: name the formats (`m ≥ 3`); give the overflow by `Specials`.
- **NEW** wording — `docs/rules/num.md`, "Exact operations need no rule", names `tl.sqrt_rn` among the operations and goes on "Their error bounds are proved: `add_err` … `cvt_err`". No lemma bounds `FloatV.sqrtRn`, and nothing at all is stated about `Format.sqrtMag` (only the evidence shows it rounds `√x` to nearest). No pinned tutorial uses `tl.sqrt_rn`. Fix: add `FloatV.sqrtRn_err` (with a `roundRat` characterisation of `sqrtMag`), or say `tl.sqrt_rn` has no error lemma yet.
- **NEW** gap — `replay_err` (`lean/TVA/Lemmas/SharedAcc.lean`) bounds a float cell with `n = l.length`, the whole replayed log. `shared` gives one replay of every program's log over every cell, so each step on another cell must enter as an approximate addition of `0` (`stepRel_other`), adding to `n` and to the absolute term, and no lemma restricts a replay to the steps that touch one cell. For split-K over `M·N` cells and `S` splits the bound carries `(1+2^-23)^(M·N·S) − 1`, useless for any real tile; the module docstring's "`n` the number of steps" reads as the steps on the cell. Fix: a lemma that a cell's value after a replay is its value after the sub-replay of the steps that touch it (or count only those in `replay_err`). This belongs with L34's round-14 rework of `replay_err`.
- **NEW** wording — Names and docstrings in the proof kit that do not match their statements (each checked against the code): `lean/TVA/Lemmas/Access.lean:12` cites `accBytes_of` (it is `Buf.Addrs.accBytes`), and `:17` promises "a row of a matrix, a 2-D tile" in `TVA.Lemmas.Block`, which has only the 1-D pattern; `lean/TVA/Lemmas/Shared.lean:36` cites `safeA_atomic1` (it is `SafeA.atomic1`), and `:32` says "bitwise" updates are determined, with no lemma for `atomic_and`/`or`/`xor`; `SafeA.atomic1`, `SafeA.atomic` and `safe_atomic_buf` say they apply to `tl.atomic_add`, which is `.env fun e => atomicWith …` and needs an `env` step first (the other atomics apply directly); `safe_upToNaN` (`lean/TVA/Lemmas/Ops.lean:143`) lists `cvt`, but `tl.cvt` is a `pick` around `upToNaN` (`safe_cvt` is the rule); `safe_dot_add`'s docstring lists two hypotheses its statement does not take; `safe_sum_bcast_mul` says "each element" chooses its form, while the statement chooses one form for the whole tensor (stronger); `foldl_max_toInt`'s docstring states the replay result, but the lemma is only the `toInt` fold identity; `lean/TVA/Lemmas/Int.lean`'s docstring says "casts keep their value" (no lemma about `IntV.cast`) and `lt_eq`, `le_eq` hold "(always)" (signed only); `lean/TVA/Lemmas/Claim.lean`'s docstring says "both lemmas need validity" and omits `hdebug`; `docs/LIBRARY.md`'s `Independent` row says "final memory determined" (only as far as each program's `Q` says). Fix: correct each text.
