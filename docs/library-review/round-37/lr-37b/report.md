## New issues

- **NEW** wording — The open question Q7 in docs/review-questions.md (lines 236-244) describes the wrong 16-bit atomics for the target. It says "the pre-sm_90 bf16 atomic is a 32-bit CAS loop" and that contiguous fp16 lanes "may become one 32-bit `f16x2` atomic". On the H200 with Triton 3.8.0, a lane of a `float16` or `bfloat16` `atomic_add`, and of a 16-bit `atomic_cas`, compiles to a 32-bit `ATOMG.E.CAS` loop on the aligned word (`work/sass_dump.py`: `LOP3 … 0xfffffffd`, `HADD2`, `PRMT`, `ATOMG.E.CAS.STRONG.GPU`, retry), which rewrites the neighbouring half. Contiguous unmasked lanes instead become `atom….add.noftz.v8.f16`/`.v8.bf16` (`ATOMG.E.ADD.F16`/`.BF16`). In `work/half_atomics.py`, neither form gave a wrong value: 0 of 1024 lanes per form off one IEEE addition. With 32 programs adding on even halves while 32 others store plain values to the odd halves, 0 of 40960 cells were wrong over 20 launches per format. Fix: describe the sm_90 forms in Q7 and cite this evidence. The trust it describes is unchanged.
- **NEW** wording — The "Atomics" section of SEMANTICS.md does not say that the operand of `atomic_add`/`max`/`min`/`and`/`or`/`xor`/`xchg` is converted to the pointer's element type with `semantic.cast`'s default rounding (`semantic.py:1278`, as for `tl.store`). Only line 1144 mentions it, in passing. Nor does it say that `atomic_cas` converts nothing (`semantic.py:1257-1263`, `core.py:2746-2751`), so operands of another type than the cell are rejected on the host. Examples: `tl.atomic_cas(p_int64, 0, 1)` and a `float16` cell with Python floats ("ptr type matches cmp type", `work/atomic_adv.py` V). On the device, a `float32` operand added into `float16`/`bfloat16`/`int32` cells matched round-to-nearest-even and truncation on 64 of 64 lanes (65520 → `inf`). Fix: state both in "Atomics".
- **NEW** wording — SEMANTICS.md "`/` on floats" gives `tl.softmax`'s result "for each format of `x`" but omits `float8e5` and `float8e4nv`. These compile, return `float32` and end in `div.full.f32` by default or `div.rn.f32` with `True` (`work/softmax_fp8.py`), like the 16-bit formats. The general product rule already covers them. Fix: list the fp8 formats with the 16-bit ones in both places.
- **NEW** wording — S3 in docs/review-questions.md (line 22) says Triton implements float `atomic_max`/`atomic_min` "as signed max or unsigned min". That holds for `atomic_max` only: `atomic_min` uses `min.s32`/`max.u32`. Its line references (`semantic.py:1315-1335`, `1272-1275`) also do not span the two functions (lines 1299-1374) or the checks (lines 1272-1275 are the `bf16` check's tail). Fix: say "signed max or unsigned min (max); signed min or unsigned max (min)", and cite lines 1271-1275 and 1299-1374.
