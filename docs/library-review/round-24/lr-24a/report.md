## New issues

- **NEW** wording — In `docs/rules/num.md`, "`tl.dot` of fp8, chunk by chunk" ("How a kernel uses them"), an accumulator fed back once per block "still compounds by up to about `1 + 2^-7 + 2^-12` per chunk, as the device's window allows (below), not by slack in the fact". ISSUES.md L124 says the same. By the page's own derivation the device loses less than `33·(1 + 2^-13)·2^-13·Mj ≈ 2^-7.96·Mj` per chunk, so `rhoChunk8 = 2^-7` is close to twice that (`work/Check8.lean`). And since every term and the sum are truncated toward zero, the device's partial values never exceed `|acc| + Σ|a·b|`, so the device's error grows at most linearly in the chunks. The fact's bound compounds only because each step is relative to the partial value. Part of the compounding is the fact's margin and form, not the device. Fix: say that `2^-7` keeps a factor of about 2 over the device's worst chunk and that the compounding comes from the step being relative to `|s j|`. If a kernel needs a long fed-back chain, a chunk fact that also states `|s j| ≤ |acc| + Σ_{i<j} Σ_chunk |a·b|` (the same bit-exact model supports it, as it supports the 16-bit derivation's "no growth") would remove the compounding.
- **NEW** wording — `evidence/num-dot-chunk/README.md` ("What Triton runs") says `input_precision` makes no difference for fp8 operands "(evidence/num-dot Part 3, bit for bit)". That Part ran only `float8e4nv × float8e4nv` and `float8e5 × float8e5`, on random normal inputs, so it says nothing of the two mixed pairs that `DotE4m3E5m2Chunk` and `DotE5m2E4m3Chunk` cover for every precision. The facts are true: the PTX is identical for all five values, for all four pairs, on both paths (`work/adv8.py`, Part P). Fix: say what Part 3 covered, and add a check for the mixed pairs (a PTX comparison suffices) to the evidence.
