## New issues

- **NEW** soundness — **Arithmetic in an fp8 dtype is not always rejected on the host.** `docs/SEMANTICS.md` says so twice: "Constants", the fp8 bullet ("With an fp8 tensor an arithmetic operator is rejected anyway"), and "Operators and promotion", its last item ("Arithmetic on two operands of the same fp8 dtype type-checks, but the backend rejects it on the host"). MLIR's `arith` folders run before the backend: with an fp8 tensor `x` of either dtype, `x * 1.0`, `1.0 * x`, `x * 1`, `x / 1.0` and `x - 0.0` compile and run, storing `x` unchanged on all 256 patterns, and arithmetic on two fp8 constants is computed at compile time and runs (`tl.full([256], 1.5, d) * tl.full([256], 2.0, d)` stores `3.0`; `tl.full([256], 1.5, d) + 0.5` stores `2.0`). The other forms tested (`x + 0.0`, `x * 2.0`, `x * -1.0`, `x + x`, `x * x`, `x - x`, `x / 2.0`, `-x`) are rejected (`work/fp8_arith.py`, `work/fp8_arith.out`). A model that follows SEMANTICS gives such a launch no outcome, while the launch runs and writes. For example, a claim about `x * SCALE`, with an fp8 `x` and `SCALE: tl.constexpr`, that models every `SCALE` as rejected is false at `SCALE = 1.0`. Fix (small): put every operator whose computation dtype (`TVA.Lang.Promote`, `computationType?`) is an fp8 dtype, and unary `-` on an fp8 tensor, outside the supported subset ("The supported subset", Operators, and its list of excluded forms), with a row in exclusions.md citing this work file. Replace the two sentences with: "the backend rejects it on the host, except where MLIR's folders remove the operation first (`x * 1`, `1 * x`, `x / 1` and `x - 0` give `x`; an operation on two fp8 constants is computed at compile time); none of these forms is in the subset". The in-scope tutorials convert fp8 values before any arithmetic, so no in-scope kernel loses its claim.
- **NEW** wording — `docs/rules/num.md`, "Facts the library states", the paragraph under the table: "The conversion and division facts are exact (bit for bit, up to NaN payload)". `DivFull` is a division fact and is not exact: it is `div.full.f32`'s bound, with margin, at most `0.651` of it (my `work/div_probe.py`: `0.645` on 2.1·10^8 adversarial pairs). A reader could take `float32` `/` as exact. Fix: "The conversion facts (`Cvt…`, `CvtSat…`) and `DivRn P .binary64` are exact (bit for bit, up to NaN payload); `DivFull` keeps a margin like the others (0.651 of its bound)".
