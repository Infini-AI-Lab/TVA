Their inputs: subnormal operands against large partners at random places, both operands subnormal, cancelling pairs with mixed-sign terms at the window's unit, accumulators against products of the opposite sign, and random bit patterns. They also place `+B`, `−B` and a small term at random `k` to test which `k` share an instruction and in what order (`K = 256`, every operand form, 4 and 8 warps), and they run the extremes: an accumulator at the room's edge near the `float32` maximum (truncation never overflows), `bfloat16` products near `2^127`, subnormal `float32` results.
