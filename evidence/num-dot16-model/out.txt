### G2: the fp8 chunk facts on `mma.sync` with a subnormal `float8e5` operand

On `mma.sync` Triton converts fp8 operands to `float16` (a subnormal `float8e5` value is a subnormal `float16` value) and runs two `float16` instructions per chunk of 32. One product per chunk of 16 has a subnormal `float8e5` operand and a large partner, so its field is the smallest normal's; the other products lie just below that `float16` window's unit and are lost (only `float8e5` by `float8e5` has normal products that small). The error against `DotE5m2Chunk`'s implied interval, on `16 x 16` with 1 warp.

### G3: `tl.dot_scaled` (`"e4m3"` by `"e4m3"`) on window inputs
